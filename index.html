02 — Projects
Things I've Built

CLeAR-VAE & CLeAR-LDM

NeurIPS 2026 Submission · Supervised by Prof. Matthew Engelhard
Asymmetric Architecture Contrastive Loss Perceiver-SPADE Disentanglement Latent Diffusion

Proposed a weakly-supervised framework for content–style disentanglement built on two key ideas: an asymmetric Perceiver-SPADE architecture that gives content and style each their own inductive bias, and an elegant contrastive learning objective that drives separation without requiring fine-grained labels. The only method (among 6 baselines) to exceed 90% on both content and style swap accuracy, with a 3M-parameter encoder competitive against 8M-param baselines. Extended to a latent diffusion model (CLeAR-LDM) for controllable generation — synthesizing content-preserving images across unseen domains.

Distributionally Robust Prediction under Shift

Individual Research · Supervised by Prof. Matthew Engelhard
DRO Optimal Transport PyTorch Optuna

Built LSTM, MLP, and logistic regression models in PyTorch with Optuna hyperparameter tuning to predict outcomes from longitudinal data. Applied distributionally robust optimization with optimal transport penalties to improve generalization under site-level distribution shifts. Evaluated robustness with AUC, subgroup analysis, and stability metrics under label noise and covariate shift.

Time-Series Prediction & Robust Backtesting

Individual Project
Walk-Forward Validation Monte Carlo Leakage Auditing Risk Control

Built a monthly time-series prediction pipeline using Logistic Regression and ElasticNet ensembles with volatility, macro, and momentum signals. Designed strict walk-forward validation and leakage-auditing procedures, identifying and fixing four sources of look-ahead bias. Developed Monte Carlo stress-testing with Student-t tails and adaptive risk-control modules for stable out-of-sample performance under tail-risk scenarios.
